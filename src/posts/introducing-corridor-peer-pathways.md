## Recovery Was Never Meant to Be Proprietary 🌱

At many points in my life, I was "fit" to disappear quietly. Instead, I found a path — one that didn't cut me when I stepped onto it. **Corridor Peer Pathways** exists so that path doesn't vanish behind me.

[Corridor Peer Pathways](https://github.com/MarkusBitterman/Corridor-Peer-Pathways) is an open-source, community-maintained peer support resource for the **I-380 Corridor** in Eastern Iowa: Iowa City, Coralville, North Liberty, Cedar Rapids, Marion, and the communities between them.

## What It Is 🧭

This isn't a startup pitch. It's not a walled garden. It's **a map left out in the open**.

The repository contains:

- **A peer support philosophy** grounded in lived experience
- **A local-first resource directory** for Johnson & Linn Counties — 70+ verified organizations covering housing, food, behavioral health, employment, transportation, and more
- **"Golden Nuggets"** — field-tested peer support lessons from real-world moments
- **Templates and structures** anyone can reuse, remix, or fork for their own community

## Why Open Source? 🔓

Because the same values that make great software also make great peer support:

- **Transparency** — no hidden agendas, no gatekeeping
- **Collaboration** — the community builds it together
- **Accessibility** — free to use, free to fork, free to localize
- **Living documentation** — it grows and evolves with the community

The open source ethos — freely shared knowledge, honest collaboration, and building in public — maps beautifully onto peer support philosophy. Recovery resources shouldn't be locked behind paywalls or institutional gatekeepers. They should be inspectable, forkable, and owned by the people who need them.

## Peer Support, Explained Plainly 🤝

Peer support is grounded in **lived experience**, built on **trust, dignity, and mutual respect**.

What peer support **does**:

- Goal-setting and accountability
- Skill-building and confidence-building
- Resource navigation (local, real, current)
- Community reintegration
- Walking alongside — not directing from above

What it **does not do**:

- Clinical therapy
- Diagnosis or medical advice
- Legal services
- Emergency crisis response

This clarity protects both peers and participants.

## Golden Nuggets 🪛

One of my favorite parts of the project: **Golden Nuggets** are short, practical peer support frameworks derived from everyday moments. Each one takes a real situation — often humorous or frustrating — and extracts a reusable model for emotional regulation, problem-solving, and forward momentum.

Current nuggets include:

- **The Incredible Vanishing Screwdriver** — A framework for emotional regulation and Plan B thinking when things don't go as expected
- **Emotional Regulation & Driving a Bus** — Understanding emotions as passengers, not drivers, on your life's journey
- **Developing Great Bedtime Rituals** — Building portable self-care routines that work anywhere you sleep

These draw from lived experience to help us live with mindfulness and greater understanding.

## Fork It. Localize It. Build Something Kinder. 🌍

If you're doing peer support work in your own community, **Corridor Peer Pathways is designed to be cloned**. Change the county names, update the phone numbers, add your own Golden Nuggets. The structure is the gift.
